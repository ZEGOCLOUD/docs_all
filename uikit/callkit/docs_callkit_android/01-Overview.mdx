## Pricing

Call invitations of the Call Kit are provided by [In-app Chat (ZIM)](https://www.zegocloud.com/docs/zim-android/introduction/overview) and are **not included in the audio/video package**. They are billed by MAU (Monthly Active Users). The ZIM free edition includes 100 MAU per month. Once that MAU quota is exhausted (usually error code 6000016), call invitations cannot be sent or received.

<Warning title="Warning">
If you reach the MAU limit or your app is about to go live, contact Sales to upgrade to the Signaling edition or a higher plan. For billing beyond 100 MAU, refer to [In-app Chat Pricing](https://www.zegocloud.com/docs/zim-android/introduction/pricing).
</Warning>
