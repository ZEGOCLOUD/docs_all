# Performance Data

- - -


#### Test Host Configuration

- Alibaba Cloud ECS instance: ecs.c6.2xlarge
- 8 vCPUs, 16 GB of memory
- Disk I/O: 106 MB/s
- Bandwidth: 100 Mbps

#### Test Conditions

- Publish 1 single stream with a resolution of 360 * 180, a frame rate of 15 fps, and a bitrate of 300 kbps to a room, and perform single-stream recording.
- Publish 2 single streams with a resolution of 360 * 180, a frame rate of 15 fps, and a bitrate of 300 kbps to a room, and perform mixed-stream recording, with the mixed-stream output video having a resolution of 640 * 360, a frame rate of 15 fps, and a bitrate of 600 kbps.

#### Concurrent Recording Performance

|Recording Mode|Test Result|Recommendation|
|-|-|-|
|Audio/video single-stream recording|When concurrently recording 350 rooms, stuttering occurs in the recorded video files.|It is recommended to concurrently record 300 rooms.|
|2-stream audio/video mixed-stream recording|When concurrently recording 45 rooms, CPU usage is 75%.|It is recommended to concurrently record 40 rooms.|
|2-stream audio-only mixed-stream recording|When concurrently recording 230 rooms, CPU usage is 85%.|It is recommended to concurrently record 200 rooms.|

#### Conclusion

Based on the test host above:

- The performance bottleneck of single-stream recording mainly comes from bandwidth. If more concurrent single-stream recordings are needed, greater bandwidth is required. Meanwhile, pay attention to disk I/O usage.
- The performance bottleneck of mixed-stream recording mainly comes from the CPU. If more concurrent mixed-stream recordings are needed, a higher-performance CPU is required.
