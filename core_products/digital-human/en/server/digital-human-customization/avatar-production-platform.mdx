## 1. Platform Overview

In the process of implementing Digital Human services, avatar production often involves multiple steps such as material preparation, requirement communication, and effect confirmation. The ZEGOCLOUD Digital Human Avatar Production Platform, which will be launched soon, integrates these steps into a clear and coherent online workflow, helping you create and manage your exclusive Digital Human avatars more conveniently.

## 2. How to Create a Digital Human Avatar

Six simple steps to complete avatar customization on your own.

<Steps>
<Step title="Fill in the basic information and specify the avatar usage scope">
Set a name for your avatar and specify the AppIDs that can use it. One production can be used for a long time, and the same avatar can be called by multiple AppIDs, so you do not need to produce it repeatedly for different apps.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the avatar type that suits you">
We offer two styles: real-person and cartoon, each supporting both half-body and close-up effects. You can choose the appropriate avatar type based on the character's features and your business scenario.

- Half-body avatars support the customization of directional actions such as finger heart, thumbs-up, and greeting, making the character more expressive and vivid
- Close-up avatars only show the part above the chest, looking more formal, suitable for scenarios such as news, education, and content presentation
</Step>
<Step title="Upload a photo to quickly complete material preparation">
Depending on the selected avatar type, you only need to upload a clear portrait photo. No need to adjust the image aspect ratio in advance. The platform will guide you through cropping and quality checks to quickly obtain materials that meet production requirements.
</Step>
<Step title="Preview the avatar effect and confirm the final presentation">
Before official production, the platform first generates a silent preview video to help you confirm in advance the facial resemblance, movement naturalness, and frame composition. Start the customization only after you are satisfied with the effect, avoiding the situation where the result does not meet expectations after production is completed, and reducing unnecessary production costs.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose actions as needed to enrich character expression">
You can choose actions such as finger heart, thumbs-up, and pointing based on your business scenario, allowing the Digital Human to make corresponding expressions during interactive conversations or real-time broadcasting, making the character more natural and vivid.
</Step>
<Step title="Submit the production and manage your avatars in one place">
After confirming the effect and authorization, you can submit the production, and the system will automatically complete the subsequent processes. After the production is completed, you can view the effect and obtain avatar information in the Avatar Center, and use it in your business scenarios.
</Step>
</Steps>
